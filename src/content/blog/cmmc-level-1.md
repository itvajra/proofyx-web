## Understanding CMMC 2.0 Level 1

The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) 2.0 Level 1, focused on **Foundational** security, is a requirement for many Department of Defense (DoD) contractors who handle Federal Contract Information (FCI).

### The 17 Basic Safeguarding Requirements

Level 1 consists of 15 security requirements specified in FAR 52.204-21 and 17 practices mapped to NIST SP 800-171. These are basic cybersecurity practices that every organization should have in place, such as:

- **Access Control:** Limiting information system access to authorized users.
- **Identification and Authentication:** Identifying system users and processes.
- **Media Protection:** Sanitizing or destroying information system media before disposal.
- **Physical Protection:** Limiting physical access to systems and equipment.
- **System and Information Integrity:** Identifying, reporting, and correcting information flaws.

### Who Needs Level 1?

If your company handles FCI but not Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), you likely fall into Level 1. This level requires an annual self-assessment, which must be signed by a senior company official and uploaded to the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS).
